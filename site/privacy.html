Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

What's Fresh is food traceability software for small food makers. This page explains what information we collect, why, and what we do with it. The short version: we keep what we need to run your account and your traceability records, and we don't sell it.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address, business name, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password itself).
  • Your business records — the information you enter into What's Fresh: product lines, products, recipes, ingredients, suppliers, lot codes, batches, production tasks and readings.
  • Billing details — subscription status. Card payments are handled by our payment processor, Stripe; we do not see or store your card number.
  • Basic technical data — a login session so you stay signed in, and standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) used for security and troubleshooting.

Signing in with Google

If you choose "Sign in with Google," Google tells us your name, email address and whether that email is verified. We use this only to match you to your existing What's Fresh account and sign you in. We do not receive your Google password, and we do not access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data. We don't store Google access tokens. You can always sign in with an email and password instead.

Our use of information received from Google follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

How we use it

  • To run the service: store your records, produce worksheets, and trace batches forward and backward when you need to.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To contact you about your account, billing, or important changes to the service.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see your data

  • You and the people you add to your business account.
  • Partners you choose to connect. If you create an API key, any system using that key can read your account's traceability data. You can revoke a key at any time.
  • Our service providers, only as needed to run What's Fresh: our hosting provider (where the servers and database run) and Stripe for payments.
  • When the law requires it, for example a valid legal request from a government authority. Traceability records exist to support food-safety investigations; we will not share them with a regulator on your behalf unless you ask us to or the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your records while your account is open. Food traceability rules require records to be kept for a period of time, so if you close your account we can give you an export of your data first. After closing, we delete your account data within 90 days, except where we must keep something longer by law (for example, billing records).

Security

Connections to What's Fresh are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and each business can only see its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information and will tell you promptly if a breach affects your account.

Your choices

  • View and correct your records at any time in the app.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data or to delete your account.
  • Stop using Google sign-in at any time; you can also remove What's Fresh from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

What's Fresh is a business tool and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For significant changes we'll let account holders know by email.
